# Azure timetracker 2.5.0

- The ticket picker always shows three quick actions at the top: Work, Meeting and Daily stand-up, all without a ticket. Before, they only appeared when a search found nothing.
- The ticket list now uses the full height of the picker, and a long activity list scrolls on its own instead of pushing the tickets out of view.
- The mini timer now appears in the right place on Macs with screens of different resolutions, for example a Retina laptop next to an external monitor. For some people it did not appear at all.

Update checks remain every minute. This update does not automatically start, pause or stop timers, and does not change tracked time.
