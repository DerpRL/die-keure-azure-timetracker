# Azure timetracker 2.2.0

- A new layout: Today, Timesheet and Insights in a top bar, with Settings behind the gear. ⌘K searches or jumps anywhere; the running timer, Pause and Stop stay in the top bar.
- Switch ticket is one dialog: your tickets on the left, the activity and comment on the right, ready before you pick a ticket.
- Click a tracked entry in Today's "Your day" to edit it.
- Delete any offline draft, including a running offline timer, after a confirmation.
- Entries that end and start in the same minute are no longer marked as overlapping.
- Dialogs open in the middle of the window, and cards, dialogs and menus show their shadows.
- Insights keeps its period toolbar at the top while you scroll.
- Settings has "Show onboarding again"; the Figma Accessibility hint explains a grant made for another copy of the app.
- The app icon stays blue in macOS's dark icon style, and "Connected to <host>" no longer shows after every connect.

Update checks remain every minute. This update does not change tracked time or automatically start, pause or stop timers.
