# Azure timetracker 2.6.0

- The app is now available in Dutch. Choose Nederlands, English or System in Settings > Appearance & shortcuts; System follows your Mac's or PC's language. Menus, notifications and the weekly report draft follow your choice too.
- Add time by dragging: on Timesheet > Week, drag over an empty slot to create a 7pace entry with a ticket, activity and comment. Times snap to quarter hours, and you can undo the entry from Recent edits.
- The week calendar now fills the height of the window.
- The week view tells you when dragging can't add time, for example while an earlier change still needs review, or when you drag over time that hasn't passed yet.

Update checks remain every minute. This update does not automatically start, pause or stop timers, and only adds tracked time when you confirm a new entry.
