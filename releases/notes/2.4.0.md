# Azure timetracker 2.4.0

- A redesigned Settings: 13 pages under Account, Tracking, Sources and App, a search box to jump to any setting, and a save bar that names what you changed.
- Daily targets can be set to the minute, for example 7h 36m. Your current targets carry over.
- New, off by default: round the time the app saves to 7pace to the nearest quarter hour (1h 5m is saved as 1h, 1h 12m as 1h 15m). Find it in Reminders > Timer defaults.
- Meeting timers can resume the previous ticket afterwards.
- Choose when the day review comes: before your workday ends, when it ends, or the next morning.
- Show the running timer in the menu bar.
- Unpair 7pace or remove its token, reset local data while keeping unsent drafts, and save a diagnostics log without tokens.
- Weekly target presets, a holiday list with your next holiday, a recordable quick-switch shortcut and an About page.

Update checks remain every minute. This update does not automatically start, pause or stop timers. Tracked time only changes if you turn on quarter-hour rounding.
