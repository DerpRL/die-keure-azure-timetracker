# Azure timetracker 2.1.0

- A refreshed look that follows the 2.0 design more closely: day timelines without overlapping labels, Today's time and Progress side by side on the Overview, solid chart colours with daily totals on Statistics, and tidier rows, tables and tray panel.
- The Weekly report shows "Week so far" next to the draft: your total against the weekly target, the time per ticket and the time per day.
- The Time editor marks overlapping entries on its timeline and in its table.
- On a Mac with several displays, the tray panel opens on the screen whose menu bar you clicked.
- A new app icon, and a macOS installer window that shows where to drag the app.

Update checks remain every minute. This update does not change tracked time or automatically start, pause or stop timers.
