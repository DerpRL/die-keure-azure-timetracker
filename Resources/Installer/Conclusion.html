Ready to connect

  1. Open Applications → Azure timetracker.
  2. Click its app icon and elapsed timer in the macOS menu bar.
  3. Open Settings, enter your own account details, and choose Save & connect.
  4. Review the repositories you want to watch.

Quick switch: Control + Option + T.

The app does not open an overview window or show a Dock icon at launch. Calendar, microphone meeting detection and launch-at-login settings are optional.
