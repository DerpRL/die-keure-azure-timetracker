Azure timetracker

Keep 7pace tracking close to your work, from the macOS menu bar.

  • Git branch and meeting suggestions
  • Continue-or-stop prompts for 7pace time limits
  • Full-page time editor with split, merge and undo
  • Azure ticket context and acceptance criteria
  • Weekly status drafts and context-switch insights
  • Informational overlap warnings
  • Microphone-based meeting and stand-up suggestions
  • 7pace Mobile PIN pairing and automatic credential renewal
  • Pause, resume, quick switch, history and agenda
  • Interactive statistics with heatmaps, task timelines, progress charts and drag-to-zoom
  • End-of-day review and clearer, section-based navigation
  • Menu-bar elapsed time, animated timer and individual daily targets

Installs in /Applications. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.

This package contains no account tokens or personal configuration. Each user connects their own accounts after installation.

New in 1.9.0: compare periods in Statistics, adjust targets for Belgian holidays and leave, and save offline drafts for review before upload. Automatic updates remain deferred.
